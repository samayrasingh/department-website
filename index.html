Welcome to the Mathematics Department

The Department of Mathematics at Sri Venkateswara College provides students with a strong foundation in mathematical concepts, analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.

We encourage academic excellence, research, innovation and intellectual curiosity.

About Our Department

The Department of Mathematics is committed to providing quality education and developing logical and analytical abilities among students.

Students are encouraged to participate in academic discussions, mathematical competitions, research activities and collaborative learning.
